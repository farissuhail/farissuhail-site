# Golf Mk5 R32 studio · asset notices

`golf-r32-mk5-2006.glb` is original procedural study geometry generated for farissuhail.com
(transmission-lab `app/atlas/car-model.ts` / `car-exterior.ts`, exported with THREE.GLTFExporter r185).
It is illustrative — not manufacturer CAD, not a scan, and not derived from any downloaded mesh.
The accompanying research notes record that no third-party Mk5 R32 model was downloaded or used
as a base. Reference dimensions (4.246 × 1.759 × 1.465 m, 2.578 m wheelbase) come from published specs.

Lighting environments are the shared Poly Haven CC0 HDRIs in `assets/studio/environments/`
(see `assets/gt3/NOTICES.md`).
