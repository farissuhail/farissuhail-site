# Golf Mk5 R32 studio · asset notices

## Vehicle model — CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 (attribution + non-commercial + share-alike)

`golf-r32-mk5-2006-shell.glb` is an adaptation of **"Volkswagen Golf V R32" by stunner2211**,
Thingiverse thing:2973517 (https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2973517), licensed
CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/). The original is a
three-door print shell (`body.stl` + `wheel.stl`); it was obtained from the public archive.org
mirror of the Thingiverse listing, which includes the author's LICENSE.txt.

What was changed (build script: scratchpad `convert/build-r32.mjs`, Three.js in Node):
scaled to 4.246 m, floor set 0.184 m above ground as in the author's `complete.stl`,
triangles classified by position and normal into paint, glazing, grille, intake, headlamps,
tail lamps and underbody, the wheel split into rim and tyre by radius and instanced four times
at the measured axle positions, creased smooth normals, PBR materials, and studio part tags.

Under the share-alike clause this adapted GLB is itself released under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. The page
credits the author with a link. farissuhail.com is a personal, non-commercial site.

The earlier procedural study model (`golf-r32-mk5-2006.glb`, original geometry) was removed from
the site on 2026-09-12; its source remains in the transmission-lab atlas.
