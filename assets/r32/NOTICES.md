# Golf Mk5 R32 studio · asset notices

## Vehicle model

`golf-r32-mk5.glb` was supplied by the site owner, converted locally from a Racer simulator
DOF/TGA vehicle archive into glTF (83 mesh parts, 258,286 triangles, 13 textures).
Source listing: https://www.tracciontrasera.es/downloads/summary/11-volkswagen/2144-2007-volkswagen-golf-mk-v-r32

The archive's own `car.ini` credits: *"Forza to GTA SA, Convert to Racer by Racer Simulator
Portugal tem."* **No verified redistribution or commercial licence accompanies that archive**, and
no claim of original authorship is made here. Volkswagen, Golf and R32 marks belong to
Volkswagen AG. This page is a personal, non-commercial educational display; it is published at
the site owner's direction and will be taken down on request from any rights holder.

Accuracy: the body is the **three-door** Mk5 R32, not the five-door. It is a game-derived mesh,
not a scan or manufacturer CAD, and not a model of any specific individual car. The assembly was
scaled to the published Mk5 R32 dimensions (4.246 m long, 1.465 m tall, 2.578 m wheelbase);
supplemental brake discs use 345 mm front and 310 mm rear diameters with approximate blue
caliper volumes. See `model-provenance.json`. The part explosion is a visual separation, not a
mechanically correct repair sequence.
