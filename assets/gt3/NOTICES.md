Survey of alternatives (2026-09-12): every free "downloadable" 992 GT3 RS on Sketchfab is either a
game rip (e.g. Ddiaz Design's Carrera Tribute states "based on a CSR2 3d model"), a SketchUp
conversion, or a re-upload with no provenance; a CC-BY label on those confers nothing. Paid store
models of real Porsches (Valdy, SQUIR3D) are sold under Sketchfab's Editorial licence only, because
the car's design is Porsche AG's. Options are listed in the site notes; until one is chosen the page
stays a non-commercial, educational visual reference with the source credited.
