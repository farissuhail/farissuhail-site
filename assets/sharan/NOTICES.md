### What was changed from the source

The source is a 2016-model-year mod. Recorded in `model-provenance.json`:
length corrected to 4.854 m and wheelbase to 2.919 m by piecewise translation (wheels moved, not
scaled); both rear wheels dropped 4 mm onto the front wheels' ground plane; the 2016 LED loop
inserts removed and 2012-style red reflector bowls, bulb centres and a narrow clear lower strip
added behind the retained lens surfaces; chrome restored to the VW roundel, grille bars, window
mouldings and roof rails; rims left at the source topology in a neutral silver.

### Cabin

The seven-seat 2-3-2 cabin is **original work** built for this studio from period photographs and
the 2011 UK brochure. It is a reference reconstruction, not factory geometry and not a scan of any
specific car. See `cabin-provenance.md`.

### Remaining limitations

The exact 2012 factory wheel design is not claimed; rear lamp internals and the cabin are visual
approximations; small trim and screen colours may differ from an individual car; the part
explosion separates visual meshes and is not a repair sequence.
